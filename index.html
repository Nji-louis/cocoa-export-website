Cameroon Cocoa Export Process & Customs Clearance

CHOCAM S.A.R.L Ltd manages the complete cocoa export process from Cameroon, ensuring full compliance with national regulations and international trade standards for EU, Asia, and Middle East markets.

📝

Pre-Export Registration & Compliance

  • Registration with the Ministry of Trade as a licensed cocoa exporter.
  • Taxpayer card and customs exporter registration.
  • Preparation of commercial invoices and packing lists.
  • Issuance of Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, and Stuffing Certificate.
  • Independent quality inspection by accredited agencies (e.g. SGS).
💰

Export Taxes & Single Window (GUCE)

  • Submission of export documents via GUCE (Single Window for Foreign Trade).
  • Issuance of Bordereau de Taxation (BDT).
  • Payment of cocoa export duty (approximately 10% FOB value).
  • Settlement of royalties with the National Cocoa & Coffee Board (ONCC).
📂

Customs Export Declaration

  • Electronic export declaration through the SYDONIA++ system.
  • Customs validation of liquidation and fiscal documents.
  • Submission of all inspection and quality certificates.
✅

Good-to-Ship (GTS) Authorization

  • Final customs authorization for shipment.
  • Physical inspection when required before container sealing.
  • Presentation of waybill and export manifest at the port of exit.
🚢

Container Loading & Shipment

  • Container stuffing under supervision using approved procedures.
  • Issuance of Bill of Lading by the shipping line.
  • Final customs clearance prior to vessel departure.
📦

Post-Export Traceability & Reporting

  • Archiving of export documents for audits and compliance checks.
  • Traceability aligned with zero-deforestation cocoa requirements.
  • Reporting of exported volumes and quality to ONCC.

Minimum Export Order

One (1) full 20ft container — approximately 19 to 20 metric tons of cocoa beans.

Export Documents Supplied

  • Certificate of Origin
  • Phytosanitary Certificate
  • EUR.1 Movement Certificate (where applicable)
  • Quality Analysis & Moisture Test Report
  • Fumigation Certificate (if required by destination country)
